- **Question:** What are the real factory arpeggiator / gate pattern presets of the Nord Stage 4 synth (the engine's 8 presets in `synth/perf.rs` are `ASSUMED`), and how does a `.ns4y` store a pattern?

## 1. Findings

| 2 | A patch stores the **result**, not the preset index: five fields in the `0x32` Layer block (bits relative to the layer base `248 / 656 / 1064`): `pattern_on` `+138` (1), `length` `+206` (4, steps = raw + 2), `accent` `+210` (32), `gate` `+242` (32), `pan` `+274` (32); each 32-bit word is 16 two-bit steps, step n at bits `2n..2n+1` from the LSB. Gate `0 E`, `1 q`, `2 Q`, `3 .` (rest); accent `1` = accent; pan `0 C`, `1 L`, `2 R`. | EMPIRICAL. Test `test_decode_pattern_tresillo_from_factory_word` pins `0x0003cf3c` to `E..E..E..`. |

## 2. Engine change

`synth/perf.rs`: `PATTERN_PRESETS` stays 8 `ASSUMED` entries (the hardware has 29; the model, the GUI clamp at 7 and the `pattern_preset` field are unchanged). Slots 3 to 5 now hold the rhythms of finding 3 that recur unedited (9-step `E..E..E..`, 12-step `EEE.E.EEE.E.`, 4-step `.EEE`) instead of invented ones; the gate length of an on step stays 4 eighths because the meaning of `E q Q` as a duration is unmeasured. No render hash or test changed (`cargo test -p nord-core --all-targets`: 887 passed).

## 3. Implications

- The step model (2 to 16 steps, gate / accent / pan) matches the storage; the gate has **four** states (three lengths plus rest), not the engine's 0..8 eighths. A faithful mapping needs the three lengths.
- Importing an `.ns4y` with a pattern (not done: `synth_preset.rs` decodes only `arp_run`, `arp_rate`) can fill `CustomPattern` directly from the three words; no preset table is needed for that.
- The 29 presets stay unknown. Until found, the preset dial is an approximation and must stay tagged `ASSUMED`.

## 4. Follow-ups
