# Synth Engine Specification (`DSPS` — Nord Wave 2 Architecture)

## 1. Scope

Specifies the 3-layer (`Synth A`, `Synth B`, `Synth C`) 46-voice polyphonic Synth Engine derived from the Nord Wave 2 (`DSPSyn` SHARC partition).

Implemented in [`crates/nord-core/src/synth/`](../../crates/nord-core/src/synth/mod.rs).

## 2. Oscillator Categories (`OSC MODE`)

Each Synth layer selects one of four synthesis paradigms:

| Mode | Description | Control Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| **Analog (VA)** | Anti-aliased Virtual Analog waveforms (`Saw`, `Pulse/Square`, `Triangle`, `Sine`, `SuperSaw`) | Shape/PWM (`0..1`), Osc Config (`Pitch`, `Detune`, `Sync`, `Sub`, `Noise`) |
| **Wavetable** | 64 single-cycle harmonic wavetables (`NUM_WAVETABLES = 64`, 8 spectral families) with 8-band octave mipmap anti-aliasing (`MIP_MAX_HARMONICS`) and Lanczos-smoothed Fourier synthesis | Table index (`wavetable_index: 0..63`), Spectral formant/scan (`wavetable_position: 0..1`) |
| **FM (2/3-Op)** | Phase-modulation synthesis with 2/3-operator cascade (`Op3 -> Op2 -> Op1`), 16 harmonic & inharmonic carrier:modulator ratios (`FM_RATIOS`), anti-hunting modulator self-feedback, and transient decay mod envelope | Harmonic ratio (`fm_ratio`), FM Amount (`fm_amount`), Feedback (`fm_feedback`), Op3 Sub Ratio/Amount, Mod Env Amount |
| **Sample** | Lock-free zero-allocation multisample playback (`SynthSampleSound` / `LoadedSynthSample`) with root-key pitch tracking, linear loop crossfade, and 1-pole timbre filter (`Off`/`Soft`/`Bright`) | Sample zone bank, Loop crossfade (`loop_crossfade`), Timbre filter (`SampleTimbre`) |

## 3. PolyBLEP Anti-Aliased Virtual Analog Oscillator

To eliminate aliasing around discontinuous waveform edges (Sawtooth wrap at $\phi = 1.0$ and Pulse transitions at $\phi = 0, \text{pw}$) without costly oversampling, we apply the 2-point **Polynomial Band-Limited Step (`PolyBLEP`)** residual:

Let normalized phase increment be $\Delta t = f_0 / f_s \in (0, 0.5)$ and phase be $t \in [0, 1)$:

$$
\operatorname{poly\_blep}(t, \Delta t) = \begin{cases}
2\left(\dfrac{t}{\Delta t}\right) - \left(\dfrac{t}{\Delta t}\right)^2 - 1, & \text{if } t < \Delta t \\[8pt]
\left(\dfrac{t - 1}{\Delta t}\right)^2 + 2\left(\dfrac{t - 1}{\Delta t}\right) + 1, & \text{if } t > 1 - \Delta t \\[8pt]
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

- **Band-limited Sawtooth:** $y_{\text{saw}}(t) = (2t - 1) - \operatorname{poly\_blep}(t, \Delta t)$
- **Band-limited Pulse (duty $w$):** $y_{\text{pulse}}(t) = \operatorname{sgn}(w - t) + \operatorname{poly\_blep}(t, \Delta t) - \operatorname{poly\_blep}((t - w) \bmod 1, \Delta t)$


### 3.1 Firmware saw (`SawModel::Firmware`, opt-in)

VERIFIED against the SHARC oracle (`docs/research/synth-voice-control.md` section 5): the synth strip's oscillator helper `0x12501F` is a band-limited **falling** saw, not the rising one-increment saw above. With phase $p \in [0,1)$ of the sample and increment $i$ (cycles per sample):

$$
t = \begin{cases} p / i & p < 0.5 \\ (p - 1) / i & p \ge 0.5 \end{cases}, \qquad
d(t) = \begin{cases} -(1 - t/2)^2 & 0 \le t < 2 \\ +(1 + t/2)^2 & -2 < t < 0 \\ 0 & \text{else} \end{cases}, \qquad
y = g(i)\,\bigl(1 - 2p + d(t)\bigr)
$$

$g(i) = C\,(B + \operatorname{clip}(A - 2i, \pm B))$ with $A = 0.3456062$, $B = 0.1152021$, $C = 4.3401995$ (image words `0x3EB0F34D`, `0x3DEBEF11`, `0x408AE2EA`): $g = 1$ up to $i = 0.1152$ and fades linearly to silence at $i = 0.2304$ cycles per sample (5.0 kHz to 10.0 kHz at the SY frame rate of 43,402 Hz, [dsp-frame-rate](../research/dsp-frame-rate.md) s3.4: `B * F = 5000.04`, `A * F = 15000.12`; `Firmware` applies the increments at the engine rate, 5.5 kHz to 11.1 kHz at 48 kHz; `SawModel::FirmwareSy` (strip chain, section 4.2) applies the knee at the SY rate; a `SuperSaw` partner above that is silent too).

| | `Classic` (default) | `Firmware` |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | rising, `2t - 1` | **falling**, `1 - 2p` (sign inverted) |
| Step width | one increment, after the wrap | two increments, both sides, quadratic |
| High pitch | full level to Nyquist (`dt` clamp 0.49) | fade 0.1152 to 0.2304 |

`SynthLayer::saw_model` selects it for `Saw` and `SuperSaw`; `Pulse`, `Triangle`, `Sine`, wavetable, FM and sample modes are unchanged (pulse and triangle are ASSUMED to be built from the same helper, not verified). The default stays `Classic`: the helper is verified, but that the Wave `Saw` panel waveform is this helper, with this sign, is not (the strip stage that a given `osc_wave` selects is open, research doc section 9 item 2). Evidence: `synth/hw_saw.rs` tests, the 512-point grid `synth/tests_hw_saw_golden.rs` (generated by `tools/synth_saw_golden.py`, kept in sync by `tools/tests/test_synth_saw_golden.py`; float32 Rust vs closed form within 2e-6; the closed form vs the oracle within 5.9e-8).

## 4. Zero-Delay Feedback (ZDF) Filter Section

The Nord Stage 4 Synth provides 6 filter models:
1. **`Ladder24` (Classic 24 dB/oct Transistor Ladder Low-Pass):** Models the classic Moog/Diode 4-pole cascade with `Drive` and self-oscillation at `Resonance` $\to 1.0$.
2. **`LP12` (12 dB/oct Low-Pass):** 2-pole ZDF State-Variable low-pass.
3. **`LP24` (24 dB/oct Clean Low-Pass):** Dual cascaded 2-pole ZDF low-pass.
4. **`BP` (Band-Pass):** 2-pole ZDF band-pass.
5. **`HP` (High-Pass):** 2-pole ZDF high-pass.
6. **`LP+HP`:** Parallel/series dual-mode filter.

### ZDF / TPT Discretization Equations

Using pre-warped bilinear cutoff gain $g = \tan(\pi \cdot \min(f_c, 0.49 f_s) / f_s)$:
Each 1-pole trapezoidal integrator stage updates state $s_i$ with zero unit-delay error:

$$
v_i = \frac{x_i - s_i}{1 + g}, \qquad y_i = v_i + s_i, \qquad s_i \leftarrow y_i + v_i
$$

For the 4-pole Transistor Ladder (`Ladder24`) with resonance feedback $k \in [0, 4.0]$:

$$
G = \left(\frac{g}{1 + g}\right)^4, \qquad S = \sum_{m=1}^{4} \left(\frac{g}{1 + g}\right)^{4-m} \frac{s_m}{1 + g}
$$

$$
u = \frac{\tanh(\text{drive} \cdot x) - k \cdot S}{1 + k \cdot G}
$$

This resolves the 4-stage feedback loop algebraically on every sample without instability near Nyquist. Cutoff frequency (`14 Hz..32 kHz`), keyboard tracking (`kb_track`: `0`, `1/3`, `2/3`, `1/1`), and Morph Matrix modulation follow the 128-step OS v1.66 `FILTER_CUTOFF_HZ_TABLE` (`cutoff_norm_to_hz` / `cutoff_hz_to_norm`).

### 4.1 Firmware filter family (`SynthLayer::filter_engine = FilterEngine::Firmware`, opt-in)

VERIFIED bit-exact against the SHARC oracle (`docs/research/synth-stage-equations.md` section 3): the Core2 stages `0x12566E` (LP), `0x125913` (BP) and `0x125A50` (HP) are two identical resonant sections in series (four poles), trapezoidal, not a ladder. With $R = \tan(\pi f_c / f_s)$, table position $(idx, fr)$ and the 129-entry tables $A$, $B$, $C$ (`synth/fw_filter_tables.rs`, linear interpolation):

$$
c_{in} = T[idx] + (T[idx{+}1] - T[idx])\,fr,\quad P = B[\cdot],\quad N = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + R P + R^2}},\quad g = 2 R N,\quad k = P N,\quad h = 1 - \tfrac{g^2}{4} - \tfrac{g k}{2}
$$

| Stage | Input gain | Per section (`a` band state, `b` previous input, `c` low state) | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| LP `0x12566E` | `A` | $c' = c + g a;\ v = (u + b)/2 - c' - k a;\ a' = \operatorname{clip}(a + g v, \pm 1);\ b' = u$ | $(c' + c)/2$ |
| BP `0x125913` | `C` | same recursion | $a'$ |
| HP `0x125A50` | `A` | $c' = c + g a;\ v = u h - c' - k a;\ a' = \operatorname{clip}(a + g v, \pm 1)$ | $v$ |

Section 1 takes $u = c_{in} x$, section 2 the output of section 1. Nominal cutoff $f_c = f_s \operatorname{atan}(R)/\pi$, $Q = 1/P$ (0.5 to 90.5); the clip of $a$ at $\pm 1$ is the only non-linearity (no drive term). Selection: the panel `FilterModel` picks the type (`BandPass` to BP, `HighPass` to HP, all others LP; ASSUMED, the `kFltType` to stage map is open). Resonance $0..1$ maps linearly to index $0..127$ (ASSUMED, the strip word to `W28` path is open). The cutoff is guarded to $[10\ \text{Hz}, 0.49 f_s]$, non-finite input is silence, states are flushed below $10^{-24}$; the render path allocates nothing (state is 6 floats per voice and side).

| | `Zdf` (default) | `Firmware` |
|---|---|---|
| Poles | 12 or 24 dB ladder / SVF, `tanh` drive | 4 poles, two resonant sections |
| Drive | `tanh` input stage | none (clip of the band states) |
| Resonance | `k` 0..3.85 | `Q` 0.5..90.5 by table, input gain falls with the index |
| Type | per `FilterModel` | LP / BP / HP stage; the `0x1257B2` ladder-like LP is not ported |

Evidence: `tests_fw_filter_golden.rs` (3 rows x 3 stages x 48 samples, generated by `tools/synth_filter_golden.py` from the bit-exact model `tools/synth_filter_model.py`). Worst error of the float32 Rust filter against the model: coefficients 1.1e-7 relative, output words 6.0e-7 absolute, low-pass impulse spectrum against the closed form 6.3e-5 relative. Table words equal `docs/research/data/synth-filter-tables.csv` (test `tables_equal_the_research_csv`).

**Envelope rate tables** (`synth/fw_env_rates.rs`): the DSP-side coefficient of an ADR strip word is a table lookup, index = byte 3 of the word clipped to $\pm 0.5$ (-64..64): `attack_coeff` (0.0922 at -64, 4.5e-5 at 0, 1.0e-6 at +64) for the three attack words and `decay_release_coeff` (`1 - c` is 0.0683 at -64, 1.43e-4 at 0, 5.0e-6 at +64) for decay and release. The words are VERIFIED (oracle sweep); that a coefficient is the per-pass rate of a one pole and that a pass is one sample are ASSUMED, and the segment generator that consumes them is not located, so the engine envelopes still use the display times and the tables are exposed only as lookups (`attack_time_passes` runs 10.9 to 1.0e6 passes).

**Envelope generator and cutoff law** ([synth-adr-and-strip-law](../research/synth-adr-and-strip-law.md), opt-in, unwired): the generator runs in the ORG core 2 pass as a chain of records $[a, b, thr, link]$, one step per pass: $y' = y a + b$, $y' \ge thr$ (and $y' = 0$ with a restart when $thr < 0$), $y' \le 1$ with the pointer moving to `link` at the clamp. The attack record is $[1, c_A, 0]$ (linear ramp), the decay record $[1 - c_D, 0, 0.5]$ (exponential decay to the sustain level 0.5), the release record $[1 - c_R, 0, 0]$. VERIFIED bit-exact on three oracle scenarios (`synth/fw_adr_chain.rs`: 32-bit-significand register rounding, truncating store). The strip word 3 sets the cutoff coefficient: $R = \operatorname{clamp}(0.36083 + 0.0079301\,i, 0, 1.0111)$, $i$ the signed top byte of the word (clip $\pm$0x6A000000) with linear interpolation on the low 24 bits (`synth/fw_strip_cutoff.rs`, 19 oracle pairs within 3.3e-6); $f_c = f_s rctan(R) / \pi$. Open: the controller trigger protocol and time base of the generator, the knob to word map.

Not done: `0x1257B2` and `0x1253BD`, the `kFltType` to stage selection (research section 9). The default stays `Zdf` (TD-11).

## 5. Envelopes, LFO & Arpeggiator

- **Amp Envelope (ADR / ADSR):** Exponential Attack (`ENV_ATTACK_MS_TABLE`: $0.5\text{ ms}..45\text{ s}$), Decay/Sustain (`ENV_DECAY_MS_TABLE`: $3.0\text{ ms}..43\text{ s}$, index `127 = Hold/Inf`), and Release (`ENV_RELEASE_MS_TABLE`: $3.0\text{ ms}..45\text{ s}$) with velocity sensitivity switch.
- **Mod / Filter Envelope (ADR):** Dedicated bipolar/unipolar envelope modulating Osc Pitch, Osc Shape/FM, and Filter Cutoff.
- **LFO:** 5 waveforms (`Triangle`, `Saw`, `InvSaw`, `Square`, `SampleHold`), exponential rate curve (`LFO_RATE_HZ_TABLE`: $0.03\text{ Hz}..523\text{ Hz}$), routable to Pitch (Vibrato), Osc Mod, and Filter Cutoff; lockable to `MasterClock` divisions (`1/1` to `1/32T`).
  - **Hardware check (DSoundman LFO clip, [dsoundman-synth-lfo](../research/dsoundman-synth-lfo.md)):** the OLED rate is the real LFO frequency (8.2 Hz read 8.14, 16 Hz read 16.36 from the audio; VERIFIED within 2 %), the waveform page lists TRIANGLE, SAW (falling), INV SAW (rising), SQUARE, S/H, i.e. raw shapes 0..4 (VERIFIED). The filter-cutoff depth law `cutoff x (1 + 0.8 a lfo)` is much shallower than the hardware (hardware sweeps at least +1.8 / -2.7 octave at Mod Amt 4.4); the octave scale is open (OQ7). Layer level of the synth (`(v/127)^2` or linear) is open (OQ22): [dsoundman-piano-pad-layer](../research/dsoundman-piano-pad-layer.md) favours linear, weakly.
- **Gate, Pattern, KB Hold, KB Sync, Master Clock (`synth/perf.rs`, `SynthLayer::perf`; manual p.35-37):** all default off, so the default render is unchanged.
  - `ArpMode::Gate`: notes play through the normal allocator and the layer output is multiplied by a smoothed gate level. Step = arp step length; plain gate is open for the first 4/8 of each step (`ASSUMED` 50 % duty). `gate_env` (0 soft .. 1 hard) sets the one-pole time constant `tau = 0.25 * step * (1 - env) + 0.5 ms` (`ASSUMED`).
  - Pattern (`pattern_on`, `pattern_preset` 0..7): each step has a gate length in eighths of a step, 0 = rest, 2..16 steps. Gate follows the length; Arp/Poly skip rest steps and release the note after the length. Preset grids are `ASSUMED` (hardware has 29 presets; slots 3 to 5 reuse rhythms seen unedited in the factory backup, [synth-arp-patterns](../research/synth-arp-patterns.md)): no factory pattern table was found in the repo data (`.ns4y` layer blocks decode only `arp_run` and `arp_rate`, see `cbin-and-presets.md`; the firmware table is not located), and the Nord Wave 2 pages only state "30+ editable presets" and the 2..16 step range (https://www.nordkeyboards.com/products/nord-wave-2/specifications, https://manualshive.com/en/manual/clavia/nord-wave-2/2619556?page=18). Locating the table in `fw_v1.66.bin` stays open.
  - Custom pattern (`synth/pattern.rs`, `perf.custom`, `pattern_custom`): fixed arrays of 16 steps with `len` 2..16, per-step gate length (eighths, `set_step_gate`), accent (`set_step_accent`) and pan Left/Center/Right (`set_step_pan`); `customize_preset(i)` copies a preset into it. Accent adds `ACCENT_VELOCITY_BOOST` (+32, `ASSUMED`) to the arp/poly note velocity, so it is only audible with Amp/Filter velocity active (manual p.37 note); the Gate ignores accent. Pan sets `SynthVoice::pan` (constant power, `ASSUMED`; `0` leaves the mix bit-identical); in Gate mode the output is panned by the current step. Tests in `synth/tests_pattern_edit.rs`.
  - ZIG ZAG (`perf.zigzag`, `arp_order::zigzag_index`): step positions `0, 2, 1, 3, 2, 4, ...` over the arp pool (Up, Down, UpDown); no effect on Random/Poly/Gate (manual p.36).
  - Fractional RANGE (`perf.range_extra_thirds` 0..2, `arp_order::extra_notes`): the held notes within `thirds * 4` semitones of the lowest note repeat once more above `arp_octave_range` (`ASSUMED` 4 semitones per 1/3 octave, manual p.35-36 "2 octaves and a fifth"; confirm on hardware).
  - SOUND INIT (`SynthLayer::sound_init`, `Stage4Engine::sound_init(layer)`, `UndoOp::SynthInit`): rebuilds the layer from `SynthLayer::new` keeping `osc_mode`, `waveform`, `wavetable_index` and the loaded sample (manual p.37); also resets that layer's cutoff morph.
  - Standalone panel wiring (`ns4-4w9l`): ARP menu pages 1-5 drive Zig Zag, PRESET, LENGTH and the per-step Gate / Accent / Pan through `SynthParam::Pattern(PatternParam)` (`nord-gui/src/audio/edits/synth/pattern.rs`; the first edit sends `Customize(preset)` so the panel grid and the engine grid agree); the RANGE knob has ten detents (1 octave to 4 octaves in 1/3 steps) sent as `ArpRange` plus `RangeThirds`; Shift+Waveform sends `SynthEdit::SoundInit`, which calls `SynthLayer::sound_init` (no allocation: `SynthLayer::new` only clones the shared sinc-kernel `Arc`) and resets the layer's cutoff morph, but takes no Undo snapshot, unlike `Stage4Engine::sound_init`; Shift+Layer A / B send `Sustped` / `Pstick` for the three Synth layers. Aux KB LEDs and KB ZONE stay dimmed: the Aux KB menu is not driven by the panel, and zone codes only act in the opt-in `FirmwareRouting::On` mode.
  - Sample Library dials (`nord-gui` `ui_state/synth/samples.rs`): in Sample mode the Waveform page's CAT dial walks the archive folders of the backup's `.nsmp4` entries (`Samp Lib/<category>/<name>.nsmp4`, categories and samples sorted by name, ASSUMED order) and picks the first sample, the SAMPLE dial walks the category; every change queues `LoadRequest::Sample { entry, layer }` for the focused layer (newest request per layer wins) through `program_ui.requests`, handled by `sync_program_panel`. Shift+dial is the LIST view (three neighbours), Shift+left dial the INFO screen, BRIGHT is `SampleTimbre`. KEEP EDITS (hold Waveform while turning, p.29) always holds: the engine swaps only the audio and never loads a sample's own settings, so the Natural / No Dyn / Fast Atk option dial (`SAMPLE OPT`) stays dimmed (no engine field). With no backup open the dials do nothing and the OLED shows `-`.
  - KB Hold (`kb_hold`, `kb_hold_exclude` per layer): `note_off` keeps the key logically held; the held set is replaced when a new key goes down with no physical keys pressed, or released when hold is switched off. The arpeggio keeps running on held keys.
  - KB Sync (`kb_sync`): the first key after all keys were lifted restarts the arp/gate (first step fires at once), the synced LFO phase, and requests a Master Clock reset (`take_clock_reset`, applied by the engine to `master_clock.beat_phase`). Other layers keep their own sample counters, so the reset only affects beat-phase consumers (FX delay sync).
  - Master Clock sync: `Stage4Engine::render_frame` copies `master_clock.bpm` to `perf.master_bpm`. With `arp_clock_sync` the step is `beats(division) * 60 / bpm`; with `lfo_clock_sync` the LFO rate is `bpm / (60 * beats(division))`. Divisions 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32 and their triplets (`ASSUMED` list). Tests in `synth/tests_perf.rs`.
- **Arpeggiator:** `Up`, `Down`, `UpDown`, `Random`, `Poly` modes. `Poly` triggers the held chord together; the other modes allocate each step through the regular polyphonic voice allocator. `Random` uses a seeded pseudo-random sequence so rendering is deterministic. The current rate control advances on sixteenth-note steps.

## 7. Sample interpolation (hardware reference)

VERIFIED against the firmware (`docs/research/sy-dsp-map.md` section 2, `docs/research/dsp-image-hunt.md` 5.15): the synth DSP image holds six interpolation kernels of 12 taps x 256 phases at DM `0xE3400` (image offset `0x76A8`, OS v1.44 to v1.66), each `h[tap][phase] = sinc(fc * d) * kaiser(d)` with `d = tap - 5 - phase / 256`, `kaiser(d) = I0(8.5 sqrt(1 - (d / 6)^2)) / I0(8.5)`, support `|d| <= 6` (the edge `d = +6` is non-zero, `sinc(6 fc) / I0(8.5)`), and `fc = 1.100, 1.050, 1.000, 0.960, 0.925, 0.895`. Max error 5.9e-8 (one float32 ulp) over all 18,432 values, mirror symmetry bit exact, tap sum `1 / fc`, checked by `tools/ns4_sinc_check.py`. The earlier 3-term cosine window was a fit of this Kaiser window (rms 3.6e-5). The only DSP reader is the SIMD stereo 12-tap FIR at `0x1C10EE`; no DSP instruction writes the voice's kernel pointer, so the selection of a kernel from the playback ratio is an ARM-side decision (ASSUMED in `SincKernelSet::select`: the kernel whose `fc` is nearest `min(1, 1 / ratio)`; a higher ratio uses a lower `fc`; confirmed if the ARM sample-start path, literal `bank0_CODE@0xC0001BAC`, is traced). The sampler and piano path of the hardware (SMP image) uses a different 6-tap x 256-phase Q31 kernel (`docs/research/pno-dsp-map.md` section 7, `docs/research/smp-kernel-fit.md`; ASSUMED role; implemented for the piano sampler in `piano/q31_kernel.rs`), so the 12-tap float kernel is the synth path only. `crates/nord-core/src/sinc.rs` implements the kernels (`SincKernel`, `SincKernelSet` built once at construction; unit tests check the closed-form properties and nine firmware values to 2e-6); the synth `SAMPLES` oscillator now uses them (TD-08, `synth/sample_osc.rs`): `render_sample_zone_stereo` picks `SincKernelSet::for_ratio(step)` once per frame and `interp_pcm` calls `SincKernel::interpolate` for each channel, loop-crossfade tap included; `SynthLayer` holds an `Arc<SincKernelSet>` shared process-wide and built at `SynthLayer::new`, so the render path allocates nothing. The switch is the constant `SAMPLE_INTERPOLATION` (`SampleInterpolation::Sinc` or the legacy `Cubic`); the kernel-per-ratio rule stays ASSUMED. Phase rule and cutoffs are VERIFIED against the Stage B routine run on the SHARC oracle (`docs/research/oracle-vs-engine.md`): the hardware floors the fraction to the 8 phase bits (position word bits 24..17, lower bits ignored; `SincKernel::interpolate` floors the same way, it rounded to nearest before), and the table equals the decimal cutoffs evaluated in f64 (`CUTOFFS: [f64; 6]`; max table error 5.96e-8, one ulp, previously 1.19e-7 with widened f32 cutoffs). Per output frame the engine is within 1.5 ulp of the firmware (`tests/oracle_stage_b.rs`: 5.96e-8 left, 8.94e-8 right for the f32 path, 5.96e-8 with `SincKernel::interpolate_ext40`, the DSP's 40-bit accumulation, a tests-and-comparison-only mode that is bit-exact with the oracle's own table). The render path stays plain f32. The piano sampler uses the SMP 6-tap Q31 kernel (`docs/specs/piano-engine.md` section 9).

Benchmark (release, Windows, `cargo run --release -p nord-core --example render_hash -- out.bin --timing`, best of 5): interpolator alone 5.3 ns (Catmull-Rom) vs 14.0 ns (12-tap sinc) per interpolated sample; whole `SynthLayer::render_frame` with one sample voice 114.8 / 113.6 ns before and 114.8 / 116.5 ns after (notes 60 / 72, within run-to-run noise: voice filter, envelopes and timbre dominate); 4-note engine chord 2890 ns before, 3030 to 3350 ns after (noise on a shared machine). Accuracy: a 0.3 fs sine at fractional positions has at least 5x lower peak error with sinc than with Catmull-Rom (`sinc_is_the_default_and_beats_cubic_on_a_fractional_sine`). Golden dump (`examples/render_hash.rs`): engine, analog, wavetable, FM, organ and piano segments are bit-identical before and after; only the `SAMPLES` segments change (note 60, ratio 1: max diff 5e-21; other notes up to 2e-3, from the anti-alias kernels and sinc vs cubic). Floor-phase and f64-cutoff change (TD-08 oracle parity): sha256 `f8602c89d8ad2ddea9ba6a41414e1c89bc4a89609386c20a3e953de5473d4106` -> `25baa387b0219c0835094150106a35ce751b9c16b68109d2a030596e78e7b59a`; only the sample segments of notes 67, 72 and 84 differ (frames 120000 to 155990; notes 48 and 60 sit on exact phases and are bit-identical), max diff 2.9e-4 (note 67, ratio 1.5: phases floored instead of rounded, up to 1/256 sample), 3e-8 for notes 72 and 84 (cutoff precision only).
